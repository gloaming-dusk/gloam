Gloam 0.3.0 boots into Horizon, Shady's new default desktop.

- Horizon replaces the bar with a shell that lives in Afterglow's sky: the
  time written across it, workspaces as lights on the horizon with their
  windows reflected below, notifications as words in the sky, a frosted
  control center turned in 3D (click the status line) and a launcher that
  scatters the applications as stars (Super+D). Super+T still moves the
  sky, and the shell, through four hours of light.
- shady-shell is now the session's notification server.
- The launchers take input methods, and notification text wraps.
- Background and bottom layer surfaces (wallpapers, desktop clocks) are
  drawn behind the windows.
- The ISO adds brightnessctl and playerctl for the control center's
  brightness slider and media controls.
- Gloam now lives in its own repository, gloaming-dusk/gloam, and takes
  Shady as a flake input; this release follows Shady at ce8c986.

Other rices ship too: set `programs.shady.rice` to `objects`, `afterglow` or
`neon-transit` on an installed system.
